Methodology, data sources & limits
  • Data-driven factors (real yields, dollar, Fed path) are computed each run from FRED series: DFII10 (10Y TIPS yield), DTWEXBGS (Fed broad dollar index — not the ICE DXY), DGS2, DFF, DFEDTARL/U.
  • Thresholds: real yield ±10bp over 4 weeks; dollar ±1% over 4 weeks; Fed path = 2Y yield vs fed funds ±25bp (above ⇒ market pricing hikes, below ⇒ pricing cuts).
  • Impact weights (1–5): real yields 5, Fed path 5, dollar 4, central-bank buying 4, fiscal 3, geopolitics 3, live news 2 (3 if ≥3 outlets carry the story).
  • Manual factors (central banks, fiscal, geopolitics) have no free keyless data feed; they are hand-written and show a review date. ETF flows and futures positioning are not included.
  • Live news comes from Google News RSS, classified by direction-aware rules (e.g. "yields rise" ≠ "yields fall"), with duplicate stories merged. It can still mis-tag; always check the source.
  • Track record: every update appends the gold price and bias score to history, so you can see whether the bias has led price. It is a summary of drivers, not a forecast.
Informational overview only — not financial advice. Structural factors are evergreen commentary; "Live" items are auto-classified from recent headlines by direction-aware rules and can still mis-tag — verify against the linked source before treating anything here as a firm signal.
