What is moving gold

Bias over time

Net score at each update. Above the line leans bullish for gold, below leans bearish.

How to read the colors

  • Bullish: supports a higher gold price, such as falling real yields or a weaker dollar.
  • Bearish: pressures gold, such as rising yields or a stronger dollar.
  • Neutral: no clear push either way right now.
  • Price action: what gold did, not why. Shown for context and left out of the score.

Where the numbers come from

Real yields, the dollar and the Fed path are calculated from FRED data on every update. Gold spot comes from gold-api.com, and the news flow from Google News.

How the score works

Each driver has a weight from 1 to 5. The score is bullish weight minus bearish weight, divided by the total, from −100 to +100. Past ±20 the page calls a tilt. Real yields and the Fed path carry the most weight.

What it can't see

ETF flows and futures positioning are not covered. News is tagged by rules and can be wrong, so check the source. Central bank, fiscal and geopolitical items are written by hand and show their review date.

For information only, not financial advice. The score summarizes current drivers and is not a forecast.
